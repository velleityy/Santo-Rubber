- Group brochure: six capability groupings, OEM development and available elastomers including ECO.

- https://www.industrikaret.com/e/ supports sales@industrikaret.com and group history.
- https://bit.ly/PT-SOL could not be resolved by the retrieval tool. Kept only as the user-supplied company website link; content sourced from the attached PT SOL profile.

Images are extracted from supplied group and SOL profiles; no generated or stock factories/products.
Client names are typeset as text and are expressly attributed to PT SOL. No unverified group-wide client claims.
No blanket current certification claim: visitors can review quality documentation in original group PDF.
Company and facility selectors are separate, avoiding unsupported legal ownership mappings.
Contact form prepares a mailto or WhatsApp message and never stores submissions or claims delivery. Visitors review and send messages themselves.

Buildless static site in dist; browser JavaScript for filters, product dialogs, company/facility selectors and inquiry preparation. Local product photographs and downloadable PDFs/PPTX included.
